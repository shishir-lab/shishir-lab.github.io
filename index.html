About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Shah Lab at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, affiliated with the Divisions of Pulmonary, Critical Care & Sleep Medicine and Data-Driven and Digital Medicine. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC), where I was advised by Professor Elena Zheleva.

My research focuses on discovering data-driven evidence with causal inference for decision-making in health (DECIDE-Health) across clinical medicine, health policy, and health economics. My work lies at the intersection of causal inference, artificial intelligence (AI), decision science, and network science. I have a particular emphasis on causal machine learning (ML), target trial emulation, multimodal and generative AI, and causal representation learning.

A central goal of my research is to understand which treatments work, for whom, and when they may be ineffective or harmful, enabling precise and individualized care for conditions such as obstructive sleep apnea. My research has been published in leading AI/ML venues, and I actively serve as a reviewer for NeurIPS, AAAI, and major AI/ML journals. I am also a recipient of the Outstanding Graduate Teaching Assistant Award and bring prior industry experience in healthcare data engineering and big data analytics.
